Factory Workforce is a multi-tenant workforce management platform built for factory-floor realities. Owners and supervisors get full control: staff directory, daily attendance rosters, shift timings, holidays, leave policies, a Jama/Udhaar ledger, payroll previews and defaulters reports. Employees get a read-only view of their attendance, payslips and advance requests.

Key features:
- Daily attendance roster with one-tap Mark All Present
- Shift timing management with grace periods
- Ledger (Jama / Udhaar) with month-to-date summaries
- Payroll preview and wage trends
- Advance (Udhaar) request approval queue
- KYC document uploads
- Offline-friendly with background sync

Requires an account from your factory administrator to get started.
