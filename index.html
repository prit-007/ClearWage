"Factory workforce tools shouldn't be black boxes you rent monthly. ClearWage gives you the whole system — transparent and free."

Most workforce management tools are SaaS black boxes. You feed them your workers' data and hope the payroll math is right. When something breaks, you file a ticket and wait.

ClearWage is different: it's a full-stack platform you deploy yourself. Your data stays on your server, the code is readable, and every feature — from OT computation to ledger disputes — is built for factory-floor realities. No accounts to manage, no per-seat pricing, no surprises.

Three pillars

Built on principles that matter for factory workforce management.

Transparent
Every line of code is readable. Payroll logic, OT computation, attendance rules — nothing is hidden behind a proprietary API.
Self-hosted
Deploy on any Linux host with Docker. Your factory's data never leaves your network. No cloud dependency, ever.
Complete
Attendance with OT, payroll with configurable wage basis, ledger with advance requests, disputes, and real-time reporting.

What's inside

Everything a serious workforce platform needs, without the vendor lock-in.

📋

Attendance

Daily roster with bulk mark, status toggles (P/HD/L/A), OT calculation, holiday detection, and weekly-off awareness.

💰

Payroll

Period-based processing with configurable wage basis (calendar/fixed-26/fixed-30), OT multiplier, deductions, and PDF payslips.

📒

Ledger

Employee credit/debit tracking, advance requests with approval workflow, and balance sheets.

⚖️

Disputes

Employee-initiated ledger disputes with admin resolve/reject workflow. Fair and transparent.

👥

Staff Management

Directory, profiles, document uploads (KYC), onboarding wizard, and role-based access.

🕐

Shifts & Holidays

Configurable shift timings, employee assignment, tenant holiday calendar with recurring support.

📊

Reports & Dashboard

Real-time KPIs, attendance trends, daily summary, defaulters, payroll summary with charts.

🔐

Security

Firebase Auth, JWT with tenant scoping, CSRF protection, rate limiting, optimistic locking.

📱

Cross-Platform

Flutter client for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, and Web. Same experience everywhere.
